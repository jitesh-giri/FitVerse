psychology Mind-Muscle Connection

  • 01. Hinge at the hips, maintaining a neutral spine. Engage lats by pulling the bar close to your shins before initiating the pull.

Bulgarian Split Squat Unilateral Accessory
Sets 3
×
Reps 10-12 /leg

psychology Form Mechanics

  • 01. Elevate rear foot on a bench. Keep the front foot positioned so the knee doesn't track excessively past the toes.
  • 02. Descend slowly, focusing the load entirely on the front quadriceps and glutes. Keep torso slightly pitched forward for glute bias.
  • 03. Push the floor away through the front heel to return to the starting position. Maintain constant tension.
Tempo: 3-1-1-0 Rest: 90s
